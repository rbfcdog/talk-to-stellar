## Introduction

NearX and the Stellar Development Foundation present **PULSO Hackathon**, a 10-day fully online builder competition running simultaneously across Brazil, Argentina, and Colombia. Teams build on Stellar infrastructure, complete a minimum of **3 customer discovery interviews**, and deploy on **testnet**.

The top finalists from each country pitch live on **July 6** in São Paulo, Buenos Aires, and Bogotá simultaneously. The top 3 winning teams per country earn a fully sponsored trip to São Paulo for **Stellar House SP** and the **Stellar Summit** immersion.

PULSO is built for builders who are ready to ship.

## Submission requirements

Requirements are kept very balanced. To be eligible, your submission needs:

1. **An open-source repo.** A public GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket repository with your full source code and a clear `README.md` explaining what you built. The more detail, the better.
2. **Integrations on Stellar.** Your project should use integrations in a meaningful way, and it should touch Stellar — for example, on/off-ramping values, or otherwise integrating the Stellar testnet or mainnet. The integration should be **load-bearing**: it powers a real part of how the project works, rather than appearing only on a slide.
3. **A pitch-deck.** A presentation showing the most relevant aspects of your project: the problem you're solving, how your solution differs from others, and anything else worth highlighting. Do something beautiful; you might need to pitch IRL.
4. **A short demo video.** A 1–2 minute walkthrough showing what you built. It doesn't need to be heavily technical or produced — just clearly show the prototype working and explain which real-world problem you're solving. You do not have to be in the video.
5. **Customer discovery interviews.** Build it for someone to use. Who are they? What do they do? How do they solve the problem today? Get **3 interviews**, recorded, submitted on an accessible drive link.

No exhaustive mandatory framework, no bounty to apply for, no specific track to fit into (other than the local track). Just build something amazing.

- Open to participants aged **18 and above**

- Open to both early-stage teams and already-launched projects
- Each project is evaluated at its current stage

## Judging criteria

- Integration depth & technical complexity
- Impact on the Stellar ecosystem
- Customer discovery & validation
- Quality of testnet / mainnet deployment

All submissions must include evidence of at least **3 customer discovery interviews** and a **live testnet deployment** on Stellar. Mainnet deployment or live traction is a scoring advantage.
