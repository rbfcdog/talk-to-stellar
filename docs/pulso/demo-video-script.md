# TalkToStellar — 1-Minute Demo Video Script

A tight, record-ready walkthrough: **on-ramp → off-ramp → yield**. ~60 seconds.
Read the narration in **bold**; the *italic* lines tell you what to show and click.

> Tip: record each screen as a short clip, then stitch. Have a logged-in test
> account ready (email `rodtretinha@gmail.com`) so balances are populated.
> Target ~150 spoken words = ~60s at a natural pace.

---

## 0:00 – 0:10 · The problem (chat home)

*Screen: the chat interface (WhatsApp-style home / app root `/`). Show a couple
of messages in Portuguese.*

> **"In Latin America, dollar savings that actually earn yield still mean crypto
> wallets, seed phrases and gas. So we built TalkToStellar — a bank you just talk
> to. No wallet, no blockchain jargon. Watch."**

*Action: type a message like "quero receber dólar dos EUA" and send.*

---

## 0:10 – 0:25 · On-ramp — receive dollars (`/wire-onramp`)

*Screen: the **Receive dollars** page. Point to the top: "Received so far $8.87"
and the **Account holder** name.*

> **"It hands me real US bank details — routing and account number. I send a
> normal wire or ACH from my US bank, and dollars land in my account. Behind the
> scenes that's a regulated rail settling into Stellar — but I never see that."**

*Action: scroll to the deposit-account card (bank, routing, account number),
then to "Move money between accounts" to show the single account list.*

---

## 0:25 – 0:40 · Yield — make the dollars earn (`/rendimentos`)

*Screen: the **Returns** (rendimentos) page. Show the top stats: Balance, APY,
the total-graph with the gain curve.*

> **"Those idle dollars can earn. Here's my balance and the yield curve — only
> the gains, not my deposits. I tap a product, choose how much, and supply with
> one tap — no extension, no signing pop-ups."**

*Action: click the **USDC yield** product → on the panel set an amount → tap
**Supply**. Show the green "✓ Supplied" confirmation. (Optional: open **Advanced
options** for a half-second to show other markets exist.)*

---

## 0:40 – 0:55 · Off-ramp — cash out (`/usd-withdraw`)

*Screen: the **Withdraw / cash out** page.*

> **"When I want it back in my bank, the off-ramp sends dollars to my US bank
> account over ACH or wire. Same flow, in reverse — pick the destination, enter
> the amount, confirm."**

*Action: select a saved destination bank account → type an amount → show the
fee preview / confirm button.*

---

## 0:55 – 1:00 · Close (chat home)

*Screen: cut back to the chat home.*

> **"Real dollars in, real yield, real cash-out — all from a chat. That's
> TalkToStellar."**

## Recording notes

- Use **Live / Real** mode on the network toggle so balances look real (it's
  one toggle, top of the yield page).
- Keep language consistent (Portuguese on screen reads as "built for the
  market"; English narration is fine).
- Don't show the access-password gate or any operator/detail panels — keep it
  customer-facing.
- If a live supply is risky on camera, pre-fund and use a small amount (e.g.
  1 USDC) so the ✓ confirmation is genuine.
